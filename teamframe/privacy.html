Privacy Notice

Last updated: 12 October 2026

At TeamFrame, we respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

TeamFrame is owned by TAKAVEN. This notice explains how we collect, use and protect information when you visit our website, complete our HR Setup Check or contact us about our services.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry or request a demonstration, we may collect:

  • Your name and work email address

The HR Setup Check can be completed without providing contact details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to respond to enquiries, understand business requirements, recommend relevant TeamFrame services, communicate about your enquiry and maintain website security and functionality.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

3. Sharing Your Information

Information may be accessed by authorised personnel handling TeamFrame enquiries and trusted providers supporting website hosting and enquiry management.

Where information is processed internationally, appropriate safeguards will be applied where required.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to manage enquiries, maintain business records and comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Information Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable data protection laws, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information. You may also have rights to object to certain processing or withdraw consent where applicable.

For privacy-related enquiries, contact: admin@takaven.com

7. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use essential technologies required for its operation and security.

If optional analytics, advertising cookies or similar tracking technologies are introduced, this notice will be updated and any legally required choices provided.

8. Changes to This Notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version will be available on this website with the updated date.
