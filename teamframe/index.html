WHY TEAMFRAME

More than records. A way to run HR.

UAE-aware by design

Leave defaults with employee-specific entitlement overrides. Probation dates and review workflows. Document expiry tracking for Emirates ID, visa, labour card — configured with UAE context in mind.

Know what needs attention

Outstanding document requests. Onboarding tasks with owners and deadlines. Probation reviews coming due. The Control Centre shows what's unresolved and who should act on it.

Implemented for your company

Configuration, agreed data import and administrator training are part of getting TeamFrame operational. Each customer installation is separate, with customer-controlled hosting and HR data.

Completed work. Retained evidence.

Policy acknowledgements, resolved actions and completed reviews stay on record. Access to salary and private employee documents is separately controlled.
